⚙️ How Fight Betz Works

No cash gambling — it's a fighter-card game for friends & friends-of-friends. We escrow the cards so nobody welches.

1

Fund your wallet

Top up with crypto (USDC, BTC, ETH) or a bank transfer. Funds sit in your Fight Betz wallet.

2

Buy your fighter's card

Pick a fight, pick your fighter, and set the price you want to pay for their NFT card.

3

A peer buys the opponent

A friend claims the other fighter's card for the same price. Both cards go into the Fight Betz vault.

4

The cards fight — winner takes both

When the real bout settles, the losing card is transferred to the winner's holder. We take a 5% hold to run escrow. Sell cards back anytime for face value.
