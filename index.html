⚙️ How Fight Betz Works

Built for friends & friends-of-friends. We hold the cash so nobody ghosts the payout.

1

Fund your wallet

Top up with crypto (USDC, BTC, ETH) or a bank transfer. Funds sit in your Fight Betz wallet.

2

Start or match a wager

Pick a fight, pick a side, set your stake. A friend matches the other side.

3

We escrow both stakes

Both stakes are locked. Fight Betz takes a 5% hold so the pot is guaranteed — no chasing mates for money.

4

Winner gets paid out

When the result is in, the escrow auto-disburses to the winner. Withdraw to crypto or bank anytime.
